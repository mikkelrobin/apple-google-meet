Calendar Meet — macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel

1. Open the DMG and drag Calendar Meet to Applications.
2. Open Calendar Meet from Applications.
3. Import your team's Google Desktop OAuth client JSON, unless already bundled.
4. Connect Google and grant the Meet permission.
5. Enable the Calendar Meet extension in System Settings if macOS asks.
   Depending on macOS version, extensions appear under Privacy & Security >
   Extensions, or General > Login Items & Extensions.
6. Quit and reopen Apple Calendar. Create or edit an event. In Add Location or
   Video Call, choose Google Meet. A fresh Google Meet link is attached.

The app need not stay open. The Calendar extension creates meetings on demand.
No Calendar permission is requested. Meetings are created using Google Meet's
API; the extension doesn't create duplicate Google Calendar events.

UNSIGNED-PREVIEW builds are development artifacts. Login is disabled because
secure sharing with the sandboxed extension needs signed App Group provisioning.
These builds are neither Developer ID signed nor notarized. macOS may block them.
Use a signed, notarized release for colleagues. No global security changes are
required or recommended.
