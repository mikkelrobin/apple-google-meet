Calendar Meet 0.2.0 — macOS 13+, Apple Silicon and Intel

1. Open the DMG and drag Calendar Meet to Applications.
2. Open Calendar Meet. This unsigned preview has no Developer ID signature or
   Apple notarization; macOS may require you to approve this specific app in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security. Organization-managed Macs may block it.
3. If a Google client is not bundled, import your team's Desktop OAuth JSON.
4. Click Connect Google and finish sign-in with your Workspace account.
5. Enable the Calendar Meet extension in System Settings if requested, then
   fully quit and reopen Apple Calendar.
6. Create or edit an event. Choose Google Meet in Add Location or Video Call.
7. The new meeting is created automatically and its link returns to Calendar.

No Apple membership, App Group or shared Keychain entitlement is used. OAuth
credentials stay in the containing app's macOS login Keychain. The Calendar
extension receives only a Meet URL or error over a short-lived local callback.

The app can be closed between uses; the extension opens it in the background.
Complete Google setup before using the Calendar menu. The local integration
allows other apps on your Mac to request new meeting spaces; OAuth credentials
are never shared with those callers.

No calendar data, Google tokens or credentials are sent to a backend. No Calendar,
microphone or camera permission is needed by this app. Creating a Meet space does
not create an extra Google Calendar event.

Setup and exact verification limits:
