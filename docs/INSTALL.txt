Calendar Meet 0.4.2 — macOS 13+, Apple Silicon and Intel

1. Open the DMG and drag Calendar Meet to Applications.
2. Open the app and click its video symbol in the menu bar.
3. Reconnect Google once for Meet access. A team build already includes the sign-in configuration.
4. Create or edit an event in Apple Calendar and choose Google Meet manually.
5. Wait for the actual Meet link, then add guests and send normally from the same editor.

A fresh Meet link is returned before Calendar receives the conference. The app
makes no later Calendar/guest-notification updates. Google may replace the code
and email the organizer; that organizer notice is accepted for this design.

Green dot: Google signed in. Red dot: sign in/reconnect required. Sign Out removes
local login and cancels in-flight creation, without deleting existing meetings.
Launch at Login defaults on. Right-click the icon for Quit. No Dock icon.

Preview distribution uses ad-hoc signatures, without Developer ID/notarization;
macOS may require per-app approval. Managed-device policies can block installation.
No global security settings need to be disabled.
